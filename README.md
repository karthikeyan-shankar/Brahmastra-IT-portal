# Brahmastra 2026 · IT Department Portal

Event portal for the Department of Information Technology at Brahmastra 2026 — Chettinad College of Engineering & Technology.

## Events

- **Hackathon** — Build a working prototype in 12 hours (Team of 4)
- **PPT Presentation** — Present your ideas in 10 slides (2–3 members)
- **Prompt Engineering** — Craft effective AI prompts (Solo)
- **Brand Builder** — Design a brand from scratch (Team of 2–3)
